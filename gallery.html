Company Milestone

Recognized for Growth & Excellence

Shree Tools received the Growth Dealer – West award from Tectyl Oil and Chemicals India Pvt. Ltd., recognizing our performance as a prime authorized distributor for M/s Tungaloy India Pvt. Ltd, M/s. Tectyl Oil and Chemicals India Pvt. Ltd., M/s. OSG (India) Pvt. Ltd. and Heimatec India Pvt. Ltd.

TECTYL (India) entered India in 2006 to support Hyundai Motor India and other Indian and South Korean companies, producing and importing high quality metal working oils. TECTYL (India) is focused on understanding the culture and characteristics of the local industry to meet customer needs and contribute to the development of the machinery industry in India.
